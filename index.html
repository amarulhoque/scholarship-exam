How it works

From registration to certificate

  1. 01

    Register

    The school submits student details. The system issues a registration number on the spot.

  2. 02

    Sit the exam

    The exam is written on paper at the assigned centre on the announced date.

  3. 03

    Marks entered

    Examiners enter subject marks. Totals, grades and merit positions are calculated automatically.

  4. 04

    Result published

    Results open on this site. Scholarship winners can download a signed certificate.
